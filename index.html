Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PurePDF

❓ Is PurePDF really free?
Yes! PurePDF is completely free to use with no hidden fees or subscriptions. You can process unlimited files without any cost.
🔒 Are my files secure?
Absolutely. All files are processed securely and automatically deleted from our servers within 1 hour. We never store or share your data.
📱 Does it work on mobile?
Yes, PurePDF is fully responsive and works perfectly on all devices including smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
⏱️ How fast is the processing?
Most files are processed within seconds. The exact time depends on file size and complexity, but our optimized algorithms ensure minimal wait times.
🌍 What languages are supported?
PurePDF supports PDFs in all languages. Our tools work with any text encoding and preserve formatting across different languages.
💾 Is there a file size limit?
Currently, we support files up to 100MB for free users. This covers most common use cases. Contact us for larger file requirements.
